✦ Creator Brief
Based on your top-performing ads this month. Share this directly with your creator or agency.
Brand & Objective
Brand: Shesha Ayurveda — Ayurvedic skincare for modern Indian women.
Objective: Drive purchase conversions. Target ROAS: 4x+
Product focus: Nilini Hair Colour, Red Sandalwood Night Cream, Kumkumadi Day Cream
Hook (First 3 Seconds)
Open with a relatable skin/hair problem — "Tired of chemical dyes that damage your hair?" or show a before state visually. Do NOT open with the brand name or a question. Show the problem, not the product.
Story Structure
Problem → Agitate → Solution → Transformation → Offer
Show a clear before/after transformation. The product should appear by second 8–10. End with a strong CTA + price anchor.
Format & Style
Format: Vertical video (9:16), 20–35 seconds
Style: Raw UGC, hand-held camera, natural indoor/bathroom lighting
NO studio setup, NO heavy editing, NO trending audio
Audio: Creator voiceover + soft background music
Creator Profile
Female creator, 24–34 years old, Indian skin tone, speaks naturally about beauty/skincare. Must feel authentic — not an influencer read. Audience should feel like a friend is recommending it.
Offer Visibility
Show the price or offer within the first 5 seconds. Use text overlay + verbal mention. E.g. "3 for ₹1299" or "₹299 only — no chemicals." Price anchoring significantly improves CTR.
