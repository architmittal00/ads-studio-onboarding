✦ FAQ

Questions we
get all the time.

Everything you need to know about Shiprocket Studio before you get started.

Still have questions?
Talk to someone on our team — we'll walk you through the platform personally.
Contact us →
What exactly is Shiprocket Studio? +
Shiprocket Studio is a DIY marketing intelligence and creative tool. It helps merchants understand which ads are working, identify why some ads fail, discover what competitors are doing, and generate new ad creatives using AI. Think of it as a marketing analyst and creative assistant inside one dashboard — built specifically for D2C brands running Meta and Google ads.
How does Studio connect to my ad accounts? +
Studio connects through secure integrations with Meta Ads and Google Ads using read-only OAuth access. This allows Studio to fetch your campaign and creative performance data. We never post, edit, or make changes to your campaigns on your behalf — you stay fully in control.
Does Studio run or modify my ads automatically? +
No. Studio only analyzes your performance, provides insights, and surfaces recommendations. You remain fully in control of all campaigns. The AI Agent (available on the Scale plan) can suggest budget reallocations and flag underperformers — but every action requires your explicit approval before anything changes.
What is a creative audit and what does it tell me? +
A creative audit analyzes all ads running in your account and evaluates them based on performance signals — click-through rate, conversion rate, cost per purchase, engagement, and creative structure. Studio identifies your best performing creatives, underperformers, ads that are scaling, and ads showing fatigue. Each ad gets a score out of 100 so you know exactly what to scale and what to pause.
What is hook analysis? +
The hook is the first few seconds of an ad that captures attention — a strong claim, a problem statement, a visual surprise. Studio analyzes which hooks are driving performance across your creatives and your category. It tells you whether bold claim hooks, problem-solution formats, or testimonial-led openers are winning for your specific audience — so you stop guessing and start replicating what works.
How does competitor tracking work? +
Studio tracks competitor ads sourced live from the Meta Ad Library. You can see every active ad your rivals are running, the creative formats they're testing, the hooks they're using, and which products they're promoting. You can also bookmark ads, create collections, and share them with your creative team for inspiration and counter-strategy planning.
What is the 1L+ ads library? +
Studio has a library of 100,000+ ads across industries that you can browse for creative inspiration. Discover new formats, explore different hooks, and identify trending creative styles — so you never run out of ideas for what to test next. It's especially useful when you're briefing a creative team or trying to break out of a stale format.
How does AI ad creation work? +
Simply choose or upload a reference image and upload your product image. Studio's AI generates a ready-to-use ad creative — with your brand colors, tone, and format applied automatically from your Brand DNA profile. It's especially useful for small brands without designers, or performance marketers running large creative experiments who need to test many variations quickly.
What is the ad score out of 100? +
Every ad in your account gets scored based on creative structure, hook strength, messaging clarity, and visual quality. The score helps you understand how strong your ad is likely to perform before you commit more budget to it. Studio also highlights weak areas, missing elements, and specific improvements for your audience — not just a number, but a direction.
What is the bulk ad launcher? +
The bulk ad launcher lets you launch up to 50 ads at once — across Feed, Story, and Reel formats — directly to Meta. Your brand DNA is applied automatically so every creative is consistent. It's designed for brands that want to run large-scale creative experiments and find winning ads faster, without the operational overhead of setting up each ad individually.
